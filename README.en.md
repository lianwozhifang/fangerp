# FangERP · 联沃智坊新一代 ERP / MES 一体化系统

**An AI-driven ERP and MES for electronics and discrete manufacturing.**

FangERP is the AI-driven ERP/MES system from 联沃智坊. It connects sales, purchasing, inventory, production, quality and accounting, bringing enterprise management and shop-floor execution into a connected business workflow.

FangERP focuses on consumer electronics, display modules and electronics assembly. Its product goal is to reduce the effort employees need to learn enterprise software and complete everyday work.

[中文介绍](README.md) · [Product overview in Chinese](docs/product-overview.md)

## ERP and business-finance integration

FangERP's ERP capabilities cover sales and purchase documents, inventory, bills of materials, production planning, payments and accounting. Financial management includes voucher numbering, queries and links back to source business documents.

Business documents and accounting records are connected, helping finance teams trace transactions to their source.

## Manufacturing execution and traceability

FangERP supports production dispatching, shop-floor reporting, lot tracking, serial-number tracking, material loading records and finished-goods stock-in integration.

Tracking modes include lot-based production, individual serial numbers and lot-to-serial conversion. Quality workflows cover defect recording, disposition, rework and scrap. Traceability connects material lots with production lots and serial numbers.

Production reports cover daily output, work-order yield, work in process, first-pass yield, defects, material variance, operator output and on-duty hours.

The shop-floor interface uses task-oriented guidance, barcode scanning and electronic work instructions to help operators understand the next action.

## AI-driven business collaboration

FangERP brings AI into business understanding, role-based guidance and everyday collaboration. Its focus is to help employees understand business relationships, find relevant information and complete tasks with less effort.

MCP connects AI applications with business tools and data. Agent Skills organize domain knowledge and task procedures. Together, they provide a framework for connecting AI with enterprise workflows.

## Delivery and implementation

FangERP provides reliable system delivery and implementation for manufacturers, with SaaS and private deployment options. Implementation is organized around the manufacturer's processes, roles and operating environment.
